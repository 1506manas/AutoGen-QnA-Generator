Question: What is agentic AI and how does it differ from traditional AI models?
Answer: Agentic AI refers to artificial intelligence systems capable of processing information and operating autonomously to achieve specified goals. Unlike traditional AI, which typically follows predefined rules and requires user input for decision-making, agentic AI actively plans, adapts, and acts without human intervention to solve complex and multi-step tasks. Traditional AI models often handle specific tasks effectively but struggle with generalization and adapting to new situations. In contrast, agentic AI can learn from its interactions and adjust its behavior over time, allowing it to handle more dynamic and unfamiliar scenarios. Furthermore, agentic AI systems can collaborate across different areas to solve complex problems, unlike traditional AI which may operate in isolation within specific domains.
